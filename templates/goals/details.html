How goal achievement is calculated

Verified targets met divided by all approved, applicable targets. Pending evidence remains in the denominator. Shared AOP/ASP obligations count once overall and appear under both memberships. Units are never added together. Campus targets are separate from university targets.

Targets remaining are not labelled overdue unless an approved deadline has passed. Linked activity outcomes require fully recorded attendance; reported aggregates are independent approved snapshots and are never added to overlapping live records.
